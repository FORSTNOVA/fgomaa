FGO-MAA AP recovery reference

The AP recovery workflow was reviewed against Fate/Grand Automata (FGA),
commit e3fa1056aabb9edbc374e95f7b53177261ac84d1:
https://github.com/Fate-Grand-Automata/FGA
scripts/src/main/java/io/github/fate_grand_automata/scripts/modules/Refill.kt
scripts/src/main/java/io/github/fate_grand_automata/scripts/locations/Locations.kt

The local Python implementation follows the idea of recognizing a restricted
screen region before performing a recovery action. Multi-use behavior was also
reviewed in the FGA Preview documentation:
https://arthurkun21.github.io/FGA-Preview-Build/scripts/in-battle/resources/energy-refill/

FGA is MIT licensed; its original notice is retained in FGA-LICENSE.txt.
No Android accessibility service or Kotlin runtime is integrated.
The quantity-plus image is cropped from this project's verified CN emulator
screenshot, not copied from FGA. AP-gap calculation, quantity acknowledgements,
inventory checks and pending-use reservations are local FGO-MAA logic.

Resource naming differs: FGA Copper corresponds to FGO-MAA bronze
(赤铜果实, 10 AP); FGA Bronze corresponds to FGO-MAA blue (青铜果实, 40 AP).
